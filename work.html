My Work

A collection of my graphic design projects.

Arcada Theatre 100th Anniversary Hat

Designed a commemorative hat for the Arcada Theatre's 100th anniversary using Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop. I focused on color, balance, and making the anniversary logo stand out.

The Arcadian Press

Created a newspaper-style commemorative poster in Adobe Illustrator for the Arcada Theatre's 100th anniversary celebration. The design was sold at the event and uses typography, hierarchy, and a vintage newspaper layout.

Glade Candle Advertisement

Created a Glade candle advertisement for a class project using Procreate and Adobe Photoshop. I focused on color, typography, and creating a fun, inviting design that matched the product.

June Seasonal Drink Menu

Designed a seasonal drink menu for the Arcada Theatre using Adobe Illustrator and Canva. I used decorative details, typography, and a balanced layout to make the menu visually appealing and easy to read.
