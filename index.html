About Me

Hi! I'm Giuliana, a graphic design student at North Central College with a minor in Marketing. I love creating designs that are both visually appealing and meaningful. I enjoy working with typography, color, and layout to bring ideas to life.

I currently work as a graphic designer for Onesti Entertainment, where I create promotional graphics and concert posters. I enjoy exploring different styles and finding creative ways to communicate through design.
